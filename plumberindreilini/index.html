The same plan also says what happens after that, and a plumber in Dreiliņi has to take this into account already now: the internal pipes are planned so that later only the yard section changes, not everything under the floor. This is about the village of Dreiliņi in Stopiņi parish, Ropaži Municipality, not about a Riga neighbourhood across the border.

Garā iela 31 and Garā iela 16: what the detailed plans say

The detailed plan for Garā iela 31, approved on 23 February 2022, provides 26 plots for detached houses. Until connection to the municipal networks, local water supply and sewerage solutions are allowed there.

The second detailed plan is for Garā iela 16 (Ropaži Municipality Council decision No. 2668, 6 September 2023). There too the plots are meant for individual houses, and the switch from local to centralised utilities has to be made within three years after the networks are built. Two detailed plans on the same Garā iela have different deadlines, so before building it is worth reading the plan for your own plot.

When the network arrives: one year or by handover

The Garā iela 31 detailed plan sets this: once the centralised water supply and sewer networks are available, existing houses must be connected to them within one year, while new builds must be connected by the time they are handed over for use. If the network in the street is built while a house is still under construction, it can be handed over for use only already connected.

The area by Noliktavu iela and Garā iela has a local plan (binding regulations No. 23/26, in force from 3 September 2026). It requires separate sewerage to be designed: the household wastewater network apart from the rainwater network. For a house this means the design must provide two separate outlets: one for household wastewater and one for rainwater.

One year is not long for an existing house, because the service line from the plot boundary to the house has to be designed, dug and handed over to the utility. If a house already stands with a septic tank, it is worth finding out the yard route and the drain outlet now: how many metres it is to the street, whether there is paving or trees on the way, and whether the drain leaves the house deep enough.

Sources: detailed plan for Garā iela 31, Dreiliņi (likumi.lv/ta/id/330394); detailed plan for Garā iela 16 (likumi.lv/ta/id/345397); local plan by Noliktavu iela and Garā iela, binding regulations No. 23/26 (likumi.lv/ta/id/370633). Checked on 23 November 2026.

A new build that later has to meet the street pipe

A house’s internal plumbing is the same with a septic tank and with a street network; what differs is where the drain leaves the house and where the water pipe comes in. If these spots are chosen on the street side and an inspection chamber is placed at the outlet, later it is enough to reconnect the yard section instead of rebuilding the internal system. The runs to each fixture are described on the page rough-in plumbing in a private house, and connecting in a shell finish on the page finishing the plumbing in a new build.

If the house already stands and the old waste pipe has too little fall or has collapsed, it is replaced before the connection, because the new network will not solve the old problem. This is covered on the page sewer pipe replacement.

The village of Dreiliņi and its three planning areas

The origin of the water matters when choosing a filter and a water heater and planning their maintenance, so in the first conversation we ask whether the house has its own well or a street connection.

The village of Dreiliņi covers 4.74 km². Within its present boundaries 427 people lived there in 1965, 1,090 in 2006 and 2,453 in 2021, and the village grew up after the Second World War near the former Dreiliņi parish board (Rumpmuiža).

The Garā iela side, the Noliktavu iela area and the rest of the village each have their own plan, so at the start of the job we find out which of them the house is in.

Asked in Dreiliņi about the detailed plan

We have a septic tank in the Garā iela detailed plan. How long will we have to connect when the network arrives?

For an existing house in the Garā iela 31 detailed plan it is one year from the moment the networks become available, while a new build must be connected by handover for use. In the Garā iela 16 detailed plan the transition is given three years after the networks are built. What to do with the old septic tank after connecting is set by Ropaži Municipality regulations; it is worth asking when you receive the technical conditions.

The local plan by Noliktavu iela requires separate sewerage. Where should a house have its two outlets?

The local plan (binding regulations No. 23/26) requires the household wastewater network apart from the rainwater network, so a house needs two outlets, each to its own network. It is worth routing the household outlet from the bathrooms and kitchen to the side where the household sewer will be, and the rainwater outlet to where the rainwater network will take it; the exact places are set by the technical conditions and the design.

A new build at Garā iela 31 has not been handed over yet, but the street already has a network. Can the house be handed over with a septic tank?

Under the Garā iela 31 detailed plan, no: once the centralised water supply and sewer networks are available, a new build must be connected to them by handover for use, while existing houses are given one year to connect. So in a new build it is worth pointing the outlets and the inlet towards the street from the start, not towards a temporary septic tank.
