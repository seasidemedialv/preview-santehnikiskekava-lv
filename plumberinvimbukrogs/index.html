In the post-war years it formed as Ķekava’s area of individual houses by the P137 road, and it is sometimes regarded as part of Ķekava. Rebuilds and guest houses have requirements of their own in the Ķekava regulations, and they are described below.

Vimbas, Augškalns, Augšniekkalns and Daugavkrasti in the papers

The official name is Vimbukrogs, Ķekava parish, Ķekava Municipality. The other names still turn up in documents: for example, the February 2009 issue of the Ķekava parish publication says that the Odukalns iron removal station is also meant for connecting the “Vimbas” settlement to the central water supply. If the land register or an old house design says “Vimbas” or “Augškalns”, it is the same place, and either name works in the form, together with the street.

In phase two of the Cohesion Fund project (2010–2014), 4,600 m of gravity sewer with 170 connection points at property boundaries was built in the built-up part of Vimbukrogs. At the junction of Ābeļu Street and Rīgas Street there is a free-access drinking water refill station, created as a resident participatory budget project, and it is a good landmark if a house is hard to find by its number.

A post-war house in the south: rebuilding and an unknown outlet

In the village assessment (2019), low-rise buildings in Vimbukrogs are concentrated in the southern part of the village, in an area of Soviet-era individual housing. In such a house the pipes were laid in different years, some from the original build, some after extensions, and there is not always a drawing of where the sewer outlet runs under the floor.

It is for exactly this case that Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022 have point 60: if a building is rebuilt or its owner changes and the sewer outlet has no as-built documentation, a CCTV inspection of the pipes must be carried out at the owner’s expense and its results submitted to the service provider. The price of a sewer video check is set by the length of the pipe and access (an inspection point or manhole), whether the pipe has to be flushed first, and whether a recording showing the damaged spot is needed; more about the work on the page drainage.

If the check shows a collapsed section or one with no fall, it is replaced before further work: replacing sewer pipes, replacing the horizontal waste pipe to one fixture from €30 per fixture. If the house still runs on a septic tank but the street has a centralised sewer, point 7.3 of the regulations makes connection compulsory for an existing property when the decentralised system does not meet the requirements of the legislation.

Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022: point 7.3 (compulsory connection for an existing property), point 60 (CCTV inspection on rebuilding or a change of owner), point 67 (greasy wastewater in catering premises), likumi.lv/ta/id/338066, checked on 24 November 2026.

A shop, car workshops and guest houses on municipal streets

Vimbukrogs has a street network only in the built-up south-east part of the village, and these are municipal streets; the 2019 village assessment mentions a shop, car workshops and guest houses in the village. A building with a kitchen where food is prepared for guests may have a requirement that an ordinary house does not: point 67 of regulations No. 21/2022 requires public catering and food production premises to have a separate sewer for greasy wastewater and grease traps.

If grease has already built up in the pipe and water drains slowly from the kitchen sink, high-pressure flushing for a deep or recurring blockage helps: from €80 per call-out with the first hour of work.

The northern part of the village towards Alejas by the Daugava was not built up in the 2019 assessment, and the territorial plan sets rural development there and a compulsory detailed plan for the whole area. There the plan comes before the pipes, and only after it does first-fix plumbing in a new house begin.

Vimbukrogs: questions about connection

Vimbas, Augškalns or Vimbukrogs: what should I write in the request so the plumber finds the house?

The official name is Vimbukrogs, Ķekava parish. Vimbas, Augškalns, Augšniekkalns and Daugavkrasti are other names for the same place, and they can be added in brackets. The most important things are the street name and house number, for example Zvejnieku or Kraujas Street, because they let the house be found on a map.

There is a guest house next to my house in Vimbukrogs. Does it need the same sewer service pipe as a house?

If the guest house has a kitchen where food is prepared for guests, not necessarily. Point 67 of Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022 requires public catering and food production premises to have a separate sewer for greasy wastewater and grease traps. Whether the requirement applies to your building is specified by Ķekavas nami in the technical conditions.

We are rebuilding a post-war house in Vimbukrogs and the outlet has no drawing. Can Ķekavas nami ask for a video check?

Yes. Point 60 of regulations No. 21/2022 provides that on a rebuild or a change of owner, if the sewer outlet has no as-built documentation, a CCTV inspection of the pipes is carried out at the owner’s expense and the results are submitted to the service provider. The price of the check is set by the pipe length, access and whether the pipe has to be flushed first.
