The cooperative’s name is still the best address, and it usually also says who owns the street by the gate. Part of the village is an enclave inside a neighbouring municipality’s territory, but it too is Ķekava Municipality.

Cooperative names as an address

Dzērumi grew up in the Soviet years as a colony of summer houses and garden sheds by the former Dzērumi manor (Misshof), in the south of Ķekava parish on the banks of the Misa. The village is made up of the parts Teika, Rožkalni, Lāčplēsis, Radiotehnika, Tekstilnieks, Ausma, Veckalni, Aviators, Pļavas and Restaurators, and originally each was a cooperative of the employees of a separate enterprise. The cooperative’s name also points to who owns the street: most of the internal streets belong to the societies themselves. The landmarks in the village are a mobile network tower, a shop and a café.

In 2000 Dzērumi had 33 residents, in 2021 681, and in 2024 901. The village assessment of the territorial plan (2019) states that the buildings are mostly summer houses, but detached houses have also been built. On one cooperative street there can therefore be a house with water only in the warm season and the system drained in winter, and next to it a house where the water pipe must work in January too; the plumber needs to know which of them is yours. What changes in the plumbing when a summer house is lived in all year is described on the page garden house water supply all year.

The cooperative board, the street and the V27

Most of the internal streets of the garden cooperatives in Dzērumi are the property of the societies themselves, and the village is crossed by the road V27 (Misas tilts-Dzērumi). If a new service pipe or replacing an old pipe means digging up a street, you first have to talk to the cooperative board, and the agreement on the trench and reinstating the surface is worth putting in writing. If the pipe runs only across your own plot, the cooperative usually does not have to be involved, unless the articles of association say otherwise.

Water and waste in a Dzērumi house can come in different combinations: the village is not named in the Ķekavas nami tariff zones, and when we talk we first find out what exactly is in your yard. If an old drain from the house to the tank or septic tank blocks repeatedly, it is flushed: drain flushing for a deep or recurring blockage costs from €80 per call-out with the first hour of work, clearing a blockage in the outdoor stretch from €50.

The enclave: Ķekava Municipality inside a neighbour's territory

The village assessment states that the south-eastern part of Dzērumi forms an enclave inside the territory of a neighbouring municipality, but it is still the territory of Ķekava Municipality. So a house in the enclave falls under the Ķekava Municipality rules on decentralised sewage, not the neighbouring municipality’s, even though the road to it leads through another municipality.

A septic tank, holding tank or biological unit is registered with Ķekava Municipality. Point 31 of Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 20/2022 provides for an initial registration confirmation on the form in annex 3. According to the kekava.lv page, it can be submitted by e-mail with an e-signature or scanned, in person at the customer centre in Ķekava, Baloži or Baldone, or sent by post.

Sources: Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 20/2022, point 31 (likumi.lv/ta/id/337167); the kekava.lv page on registering decentralised sewage; the village assessment (2019). Checked on 24 November 2026.

The Misa 10% zone and the utility room

In Dzērumi some of the land lies in the area with a 10% flood probability set for the Misa river, and the village assessment names it as a building restriction. Whether your own plot falls within it is shown by the territorial plan.

For plumbing this matters if a water heater, a water inlet or a pump stands in a sunken utility room or a basement. Then before a rebuild you decide where the water would go from that room if it appears there, and whether the equipment can be raised higher. There is a page on this: water in the basement.

What people in Dzērumi ask

The utility room in Dzērumi is below ground level in the Misa 10% zone. What should be decided before a water heater or pump is moved there?

First, where the water would go if it appears in the room, and whether the equipment can be placed higher than the floor. The village assessment names the Misa 10% flood probability area as a building restriction in Dzērumi, and whether it affects your plot is shown by the territorial plan. For work when water is already in the room, see the page water in the basement.

The cooperative board in Dzērumi requires the street to be restored after digging. What should be agreed before the work?

Where the trench will run and how wide it will be, when the work will happen, who restores the surface and in what layers. Most of the internal streets of the Dzērumi cooperatives are the property of the societies themselves, so this agreement is worth putting in writing before digging starts.

How do I register a septic tank in Dzērumi if the house is still a summer house on paper?

The registration confirmation is submitted on the form in annex 3 of binding regulations No. 20/2022 by e-mail, in person in Ķekava, Baloži or Baldone, or by post. The form asks about the sewage system, not the house’s status, but if you are unsure whether the status matters in your case, ask the municipality before submitting.
