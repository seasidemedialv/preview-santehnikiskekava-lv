Ziedonis on paper and on the map

Ziedonis is one of the three older places that, together with the new areas of Akmeņsala, were made into the village of Katlakalns. In the village assessment of the territorial plan in 2019, Ziedonis is described as the dense north-eastern part of Katlakalns, dominated by detached houses, with its streets owned by the municipality.

Ziedonis grew up as a cooperative garden society, so a house may have begun as a garden house that was later rebuilt for year-round living. Before connecting to the street network, it is worth looking at the internal pipes of such a house too, so that the new supply line is not joined to a pipe that will soon be replaced anyway. What to change when a garden house starts to be lived in through the winter is described on the page garden house water supply all year.

In the form you may write either “Ziedonis” or “Katlakalns”; the main thing is the street. A landmark for those coming from Riga is the terminus of Rīgas satiksme bus No. 12, which is right in Ziedonis, as are the Katlakalns cemetery and the Katlakalns Lutheran church (1794).

Narrow garden streets and the place for a meter assembly

The street network of Ziedonis is newer than the houses themselves: in the project “Development of water management services in Ķekava, stage 3” (2012–2015) 8,404 m of water main with fire hydrants and 8,030 m of gravity sewer with 535 connection points for private houses were built here, for example on Ozolu, Krokusu, Liliju and Egļu streets. The street network was built by Ķekavas nami in the project, and the wastewater goes on to the Ķekava-Riga pressure main. So that water could reach the village, the same stage built a new 1,450 m trunk water main to Ziedonis, and for sewerage 1,295 m of pressure main and three pumping stations; 950 m of pressure main and a pumping station by the village were built already in the earlier phase (2010–2014).

Narrow garden streets and hydrants mean that a trench in the yard has to be planned with the neighbour’s fence and the access in mind. If the meter assembly cannot be built in the place the regulations provide, point 28 of Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022 lets the service provider allow it in a water meter chamber on the yard network or indoors, if a chamber in the yard cannot be agreed with the landowners or network owners.

A Ziedonis house and the municipal co-financing

A Ziedonis house has two features that touch co-financing: the property is in Ķekava Municipality, and both a water main and a sewer have already been built along it. Binding regulations No. 14/2023 give priority to sewer connections where both networks run along the property (point 3), but the building’s internal water supply and sewer system is not included in the support (point 4.3). The conditions and amounts step by step are in the article on co-financing for a connection.

What exactly the co-financing covers is set by the Ķekavas nami estimate. If the service pipe from the boundary to the house is not part of it, the owner builds it under the technical conditions, and its price is set by length, depth and yard obstacles. Nobody pays for the inside of the house: if the water pipe in a garden house was laid for summer use only, replacing the pipes in a house helps, with new pipe in place of the old from €10/m and a supply or waste run to one fixture from €35.

Co-financing procedure: binding regulations 14/2023 with the 2026 amendments (likumi.lv/ta/id/344492), points 3 and 4.3; place of the meter assembly: binding regulations 21/2022, point 28 (likumi.lv/ta/id/338066). Viewed on 24 November 2026.

Ziedonis houses: questions

My address is Liliju iela. In the documents, is that Ziedonis or Katlakalns?

Administratively it is the village of Katlakalns in Ķekava parish, Ķekava Municipality, and Ziedonis is a part of it. In the Ķekavas nami tariffs the same place is called “Ziedoņa ciems”. In our form both names work if a street is given.

The street has both a water main and a sewer, but the house is still on a septic tank. Which connection does the municipality give priority to?

The sewer. Point 3 of binding regulations No. 14/2023 gives priority to sewer connections where both networks are built along the property, and in Ziedonis stage three built both. The water main can be connected at the same time if the technical conditions and the budget allow.

On a narrow Ziedonis garden plot there is no room for a meter chamber. Where can the meter assembly go?

Point 28 of Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022 lets the service provider allow the meter assembly elsewhere if it cannot be built in the place the regulations provide: in a water meter chamber on the yard network or indoors, if a chamber in the yard cannot be agreed with the landowners or network owners. The place is agreed with Ķekavas nami before building.
