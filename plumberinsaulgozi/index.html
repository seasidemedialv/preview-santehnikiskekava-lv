New houses here can therefore go up next to still empty plots, and for each of them the first plumbing, the purchase and the biological treatment unit are a separate matter each.

The detailed plan by Celmiņu iela: land divided, streets private

Saulgoži is a small village in Ķekava parish with an area of 1.5 km²; according to LĢIA data, 91 people lived there in 2019. The streets laid out in the detailed plan, Celmiņu, Līduma and Cirsmas iela, are privately owned, and in 2019 not all the planned streets had been fully built.

For a house on a private street this means two things. If a pipe or a trench has to run along the street, it is agreed with the street owner. And if the street is not yet built to the end, delivery of materials to the house may depend on the weather.

Finishing a new build or buying a ready one: what to check in the plumbing

If a Saulgoži new build is handed over in shell finish, the pipes and outlets under the floor are already there, but the fixtures are not. Then the work is finishing the plumbing on the existing outlets: the WC, basins, shower, water heater and manifold. The price depends on the number of fixtures, on connecting the water heater and the manifold, and on whether the outlets match the plan and have been pressure-tested. Fitting a floor-standing WC, sealing and connecting costs from €60 per WC, and a concealed frame with a wall-hung WC from €85 per set.

If you are buying a house, a plumbing inspection before purchase answers questions the seller may not know: whether the outlets are where the plan says, whether they are capped and pressure-tested, where the sewer leaves the house and what happens to it next. In a new build the last question is especially important, because the outlet usually leads to the house’s own unit, and the inspection finds that out. The price of the inspection depends on the size of the house and on whether a video check of the sewer and a written opinion are needed.

Farmsteads on the banks of the Sūnupe and the BIO unit

Saulgoži lies in the south-east of Ķekava parish on the banks of the Sūnupe, by the state regional road P89 (Ķekava-Skaistkalne). According to the village assessment (2019), it consists of forest and farmland with farmsteads, while the separate houses are concentrated in the middle of the village, where the land is divided. Saulgoži is not on the Ķekavas nami tariff page, so the first question when we talk is about the well or borehole and about where the wastewater goes.

If a house has a biological treatment unit (BIO) that discharges the treated wastewater into the municipality’s environment, it has its own obligation in Ķekava Municipality: by 1 June each year a copy of a written confirmation from a suitable company must be submitted to the municipality about the technical servicing done, the technical condition of the unit and further instructions for its use. When buying a house with a BIO unit, ask for the latest confirmation; it shows whether the unit has been serviced.

In plumbing terms a BIO unit is a living system: bacteria in it process the wastewater. In a new house it is worth knowing that the washing machine and kitchen drains also go to the BIO unit, so do not pour fat, solvents or paint down the sink. How often the sludge is removed from a BIO unit is set by the manufacturer’s documentation.

Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 20/2022, point 33.4 (BIO unit servicing confirmation by 1 June) and point 10 (sludge removal according to the manufacturer’s documentation), likumi.lv, checked on 24 November 2026.

Asked in Saulgoži by owners of new houses

A new house on Celmiņu iela has a BIO unit: what must be submitted to the municipality every year by 1 June?

A copy of a written confirmation from a suitable company about the unit’s technical servicing, its technical condition and further instructions for use. This is required by Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 20/2022 (point 33.4) for units that discharge treated wastewater into the municipality’s environment.

What should I check in the plumbing when buying a new build in the Saulgoži detailed plan?

Whether the outlets match the plan and have been pressure-tested, where the sewer leaves the house and whether it has an inspection point, what the treatment unit is and whether there is a confirmation of its servicing, and where the water in the house comes from. If the street is private, find out how the supply lines were agreed with the street owner.

The BIO unit in a Saulgoži house has no manufacturer's documentation. What decides how often the sludge is removed?

Point 10 of Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 20/2022: for a biological unit the removal frequency is set by the manufacturer’s documentation, and if there is none, by the written opinion of a competent company. The written confirmation of the unit’s technical servicing must still be submitted to the municipality every year by 1 June.
