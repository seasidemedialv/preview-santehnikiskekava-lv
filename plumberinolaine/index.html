Which parts of Olaine we serve

We accept requests from the whole town of Olaine, both from blocks of flats and from private houses. The town’s water supply networks are 20,700 m long in total, and 11,407 m of them have been renovated; the sewer networks are 22,200 m long with 9 pumping stations. In reconstruction projects, 70% of all sewer pipelines have been replaced or renovated. The new wastewater treatment plant was opened at the end of 2009, and its design capacity is 4,471 m³ a day in dry weather and up to 8,040 m³ a day in rain (data of AS “Olaines ūdens un siltums”).

It is the town network that has been renewed, not the inside of the houses. Olaine’s water management regulations SN4/2018 leave with the user the sewer service pipe as far as the first network manhole, the meter assembly and everything inside the house (points 20 and 24), unless the contract says otherwise. In a block of flats, an old cast-iron riser can therefore remain behind a new street pipe, and in a private house an old outlet up to the manhole.

Rainwater in the channel, drains in the basement

Rainwater in the town of Olaine, apart from that from the industrial zone, is not treated: in several places it runs out into the Olaine town channel. Domestic wastewater, by contrast, reaches the treatment plant. The town stands in the Tīreļi plain, and the Misa tributaries Olainīte and Pupla flow through it.

For a basement, Olaine’s regulations SN4/2018 ask for something specific in point 12. Fixtures below the level of the nearest sewer manhole cover, for example a basement floor drain, sink, shower or WC, are connected to a separate sewer with its own outlet and an electrically controlled gate valve operated by a sensor. That way wastewater cannot flow back into the basement when the level in the network rises.

If a sewage lifting pump raises the water for basement fixtures and it stops working, it is checked, repaired or replaced; the scope of work depends on the pump type and how easy it is to reach. More: sewage pump repair.

What people ask in Olaine before a call-out

Can a plumber be requested in Olaine outside working hours?

Yes, we provide emergency plumbing services in Olaine also at night and at weekends.

What jobs does a plumber take on in Olaine?

We do pipe repair and replacement, drain cleaning, installation and repair of water heaters, installation of mixer taps, shower cabins and WCs, and replacement of water supply pipes in Olaine.

The sewer networks in Olaine have mostly been renewed. So why does the riser in our building still block?

In reconstruction projects 70% of the town’s sewer pipelines were renewed, but the riser is the building’s internal system. Under point 20 of SN4/2018 it is maintained by the user at their own expense, unless otherwise provided, so street works do not change it. Clearing a drain with a cable costs from €40, and replacing a cast-iron section with plastic in one flat from €100; riser work is agreed with the building manager.

In Olaine the water meter belongs to "Olaines ūdens un siltums". What does that mean if the pipes next to it have to be changed?

Points 17 and 18 of SN4/2018 say that the verified meter is installed by AS “Olaines ūdens un siltums” and remains the company’s property, while the meter assembly and the pipes around it are the user’s (points 20 and 24). If a meter is stolen or damaged, the user covers the loss, unless the assembly is outside the user’s boundary. So work at the assembly is agreed with the company before it starts.
