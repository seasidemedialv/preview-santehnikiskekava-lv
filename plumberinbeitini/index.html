This page is about the houses around Beitiņu iela and the Loreķi side. The village named Beitiņi is elsewhere, in Stelpe parish in Bauska Municipality, and what is written here does not apply to it.

Where Beitiņi is, seen from Ķekava

On the map the farmstead “Beitiņi” (OpenStreetMap data; the place name is in the LĢIA place-names database) lies about 1.5 km south-west of the centre of Ķekava, within the village of Krogsils. Beitiņu iela leads to it, and nearby are Buku iela, Begoniju iela and Nākotnes iela, the Loreķi neighbourhood and the road from Ķekava to Ganģi.

For the plumber the street name is what matters. If you enter “Beitiņi” in the form without a street, it is not clear whether you mean the farmstead, a house on Beitiņu iela or the village in Stelpe parish. With a street and a house number, for example on Beitiņu, Buku or Nākotnes iela, the house is found on the map at once.

The village of Beitiņi, also called Noras and earlier Stelpes pienotava, is by the Misa and the P89 road in Bauska Municipality, and different municipal rules apply there.

Krogsils rules apply to Beitiņu iela too

Because Beitiņu iela is part of Krogsils, what suits houses in Krogsils suits houses here. We do not assume a network connection here beforehand, because Krogsils is not on the “Ķekavas nami” tariff list; the water of a house may come from a well or a borehole, and the waste may go to a septic tank, a holding tank or a biological unit, so we find out the system house by house. More about the streets of Krogsils, detailed plans and new builds is on the page Plumber in Krogsils.

If a house has its own sewage system, in Ķekava Municipality it has to be registered with the municipality. Registration is not for ever: changes to the house must be reported again.

Sale, rebuild or connection: notify the municipality within a month

The Ķekava Municipality regulations on decentralised sewage (point 32) list the cases when the owner must submit a registration confirmation again: the property is transferred, the system is rebuilt or its type is changed, the property is connected to the centralised sewer, the planned volume of wastewater changes, or the number of declared or actually resident people changes. The deadline is at once, but no later than within one month of the purchase or the change. The confirmation is submitted to the municipality in person, by post or electronically.

In plumbing this touches more jobs than it seems. If a septic tank is replaced with a biological unit, that is a change of the system type. If more people move into the house, the volume changes. So before a bigger rebuild it is worth thinking about the paper that has to be submitted after it.

Registration confirmation and notification of changes: Ķekava Municipality regulations on decentralised sewage No. 20/2022 (points 31, 32), likumi.lv, checked on 24 November 2026.

Cleaning and an inspection for a house with its own septic tank

If the drains in the house run slowly or the WC will not clear, the job is drain cleaning: a sink, bath, shower or WC is cleared with a cable from €40 per call-out, and a blockage in the outdoor section up to the manhole is cleared from €50 (first hour of work). In the form, write whether the drains have stopped in the whole house or only at one fixture: that shows whether the outlet of one fixture is blocked or the common pipe to the septic tank. If you are buying or selling a house, a plumbing inspection before buying is suitable: its price depends on the size of the house, a video check of the sewer and a written opinion.

For a house with its own septic tank the inspection before purchase has its own questions. Whether the septic tank is registered and whether the type given in the registration matches what stands in the yard. When it was last emptied, and whether there are receipts. Whether the pipe from the house to the septic tank runs with a fall and without dips where sediment collects. And whether the septic tank has an infiltration system, because septic tank water may be released into the environment only through it. The answers decide whether the first job after the purchase will be cleaning, a rebuild or simply emptying.

Questions about Beitiņi

My address is on Beitiņu iela: which village does it count as?

Beitiņu iela is within the village of Krogsils in Ķekava parish, Ķekava Municipality. The exact village name is shown by your address in the address register or the land register, and it can be entered in the form just so.

I am looking for a plumber in Beitiņi near Stelpe in Bauska Municipality: is this page about there?

No. This page is about Beitiņu iela in Krogsils near Ķekava. The Beitiņi in Stelpe parish is, by map data, about 40 km further away, and Bauska Municipality rules apply there. If the house is there, enter it in the form, and we clarify in the conversation whether we can help.

I am selling a house near Beitiņu iela with a septic tank: what must be reported to the municipality?

Under Ķekava Municipality regulations No. 20/2022 (point 32), a registration confirmation for the decentralised sewage system must be submitted to the municipality within one month of the purchase. The deadline counts from the acquisition, so the confirmation is submitted by the buyer as the new owner, but it is worth the seller handing over the septic tank papers and the latest emptying receipts.
