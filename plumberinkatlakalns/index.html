The borehole, the iron-removal plant and the water tower

In the same project in which Katlakalns got its street networks, a new drinking-water iron-removal plant with a capacity of 12 m³/h was built and a 170 m deep borehole was added. Ķekavas nami flushes and disinfects the Katlakalns water tower twice a year and then checks the water quality. The company prepares and checks the water in the network; the supply line from the connection point to the house and everything after the meter, including the water heater and filters, is usually the owner’s responsibility.

The place where the sample of 27 August 2026 was taken matters: it was taken after the iron-removal plant, so it shows the water leaving the plant into the network. Iron in it was below 0.04 mg/L, while the limit is 0.2 mg/L, but the plant does not remove limescale, so water heater servicing from €75 per heater remains the homeowner’s job.

Ģipšustūris and Pļavniekkalns: the village's older parts

Katlakalns was formed by joining three older settlements, Pļavniekkalns, Ģipšustūris and Ziedonis, with the new detached-house areas in Akmeņsala. Ģipšustūris, or the Lime Factory (Kaļķu fabrika), arose as a workers' village by a limestone quarry, and Rīgas satiksme bus No. 26 runs to it. Pļavniekkalns grew as a detached-house district by the former Katlakalns parish board, where there are a primary school, a community centre and the kindergarten “Bitīte”. Ziedonis has its own page.

In these parts the street networks are the work of the project’s second phase, and on the water side that is a 7,091 m network with 314 connection points on 19 streets; new networks were laid, for example, on Tulpju, Sporta, Kazeņu, Asteru, Pļavniekkalna, Ievu, Rožu and Bērzu streets. What counts as a house’s connection point is set by point 3.9 of Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022: for water it is the shut-off fitting in the service provider’s water chamber, for sewerage the street manhole where the property’s outlet joins the network. Whether there is such a point right by your plot is shown by the technical conditions.

If in an older house the ready-made yard drain between the house and the street manhole blocks, it is cleared from the inspection point or from the house side: clearing the outdoor drain costs from €50 per call-out with the first hour of work. It helps if it is known how many metres it is to the manhole and whether there is an inspection point on the way.

Akmeņsala: new houses by unfinished streets

The village assessment of the territorial plan in 2019 states that active construction of low-rise private houses is under way in Katlakalns, but most of the detailed plans have not yet been carried out and the street network is not fully in order. Houses in Akmeņsala can therefore stand next to both built streets and streets that are on the map but not yet finished on the ground.

Where the outlet leaves a new build in Akmeņsala and where the water pipe comes in is set by the Ķekavas nami technical conditions if the street has a network, and if it does not, by the position of the house’s own system on the plot. After that, water and waste are brought to each fixture position: first-fix plumbing costs from €55 per spot.

A house with a new connection also has one rule to know before it is commissioned. Point 21 of Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022 states that water from the new connection may be used before the building is accepted for operation only with the service provider’s written consent and a meter installed by it. If the building work needs water from the supply line, agree it with Ķekavas nami beforehand.

Questions from Katlakalns

In Akmeņsala the house has not yet been commissioned. May I take water from the new service pipe for building work?

Only with Ķekavas nami’s written consent and a meter installed by the company: that is what point 21 of Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022 states. Without it, water from the new connection may not be used before the building is accepted for operation, so agree it before the building work needs water.

Does a Katlakalns house on network water still need its own iron filter?

The sample taken on 27 August 2026 after the iron-removal plant showed iron below 0.04 mg/L, so the plant removes iron before the water reaches the network. A filter left in the house from the times when the water came from its own well should be maintained according to the manufacturer’s instructions or removed if it is no longer used. Limescale stays in the water, and that is a matter of water heater servicing.

Is the village of Katlakalns in Ķekava parish the same as Katlakalns in Riga?

No. This page is about the village in the north of Ķekava parish between the roads A7 and P137, the Sausā Daugava and the Daugava. Next to it in the city of Riga there is a neighbourhood of the same name, but that is a different place. The village is served by SIA “Ķekavas nami”, and Ķekava Municipality binding regulations apply in it.
