So the question of where a new water or sewer pipe may run starts here with the owner of the street, not with the trench. Krustkalni is a medium-sized village in Ķekava parish between Riga, Baloži and the A7 road, and low-rise houses and blocks of flats stand side by side in it.

Street, plot, house: what to know about a Krustkalni address

These questions decide whether the job will be a simple repair or will need agreement with neighbours. If you know the answers, write them in the request.

  1. Who owns the street by the plot If it is not Mežmalas iela or Zālītes iela, it is most likely private. Digging in the street needs the owner’s consent, and a request for technical conditions has to state also the co-owners' consent and an excavation permit, if the works affect the street.
  2. Whether the house is connected to the network Krustkalni village lies in the water and sewerage tariff zone of SIA “Ķekavas nami”, but that does not yet mean every house is connected. If the house has a contract with “Ķekavas nami” and a meter, it is on the network; if it has its own well and septic tank, the work is on its own system.
  3. Which detailed plan the plot comes from Krustkalni has about sixteen detailed plans in force, drawn up mainly to divide land for residential building. The plan shows where utilities are intended, and that helps in seeing where a pipe can run.
  4. A flat in a block or a detached house In a block of flats the risers are usually the common property of all the owners, unless the building’s documents say otherwise, and replacing them is agreed with the building manager. In a detached house everything from the connection point to the tap is the owner’s responsibility.

Co-op houses, blocks in the centre and in the north

The village assessment of 2019 states that most of the building in Krustkalni is low-rise residential houses, and some of them grew out of former garden cooperatives. In the central part and in the north by the Riga border there are blocks of flats. The population has grown from 115 (1979) to 745 (2021).

In a house that began as a garden house, the pipes were originally meant for summer, and after a rebuild they may have stayed the old ones. If the water supply has to be renewed, replacing pipes in a private house costs from €10/m for new pipe in place of the old (fixed along a wall or floor) and from €35 for a run to one fixture. In a block of flats, if water comes from the flat above, help after flooding from neighbours starts with shutting off the water and isolating the fault, from €35.

If the house is new, built under one of the detailed plans, the work is usually different: plumbing from scratch and a connection whose position is set by the technical conditions. Landmarks for those who drive here: Krustkalni borders the city of Riga, the town of Baloži and the A7 road, on the other side of the road are Valdlauči and Rāmava, and in the south-eastern part of the village there is a logistics centre of SIA “Maxima Latvija” and a petrol station.

Connection through a neighbour's pipes: what the rules allow

On private streets a direct connection to the network is not always possible. Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022 provide a way out: if the layout of the networks does not allow a direct connection, the service provider may allow connecting as an additional user to another customer’s pipes behind that customer’s meter assembly. This needs the written consent of the landowner and of that customer, and a boundary act (point 25).

In practice this means an agreement with the neighbour whose service pipe already runs to the network, and the permission of “Ķekavas nami”. Then the service pipe to the house is built from the indicated point in the neighbour’s system; the work is described on the page service line to the house. There is no single starting price for this work, because it is set by the length of the section, the depth and what has to be crossed. In the boundary act it is worth recording who maintains the shared section, so that the next repair does not begin with a dispute.

If the connection point is planned at the plot boundary, a request for technical conditions has to include more than just an application: consent to data processing, a statement that you have read the municipality’s excavation rules, the co-owners' consent and an excavation permit if the works take place in a street or protection zones (points 8 and 9). On a private street in Krustkalni the co-owners may be several neighbours, so it is worth collecting the signatures before the work is ordered.

An additional user behind a neighbour’s meter assembly (point 25) and documents for the request (points 8-9) are in the Ķekava Municipality water management rules, which we viewed on likumi.lv on 23 November 2026.

What people in Krustkalni ask

My street in Krustkalni is private. How do I connect through a neighbour's service pipe?

With two written consents and the permission of “Ķekavas nami”. Point 25 of regulations No. 21/2022 allows connecting as an additional user behind another customer’s meter assembly, if the landowner and that customer agree and a boundary act is drawn up. Then the service pipe from the neighbour’s system to your house is built like an ordinary connection.

A house in Krustkalni was built in a former garden cooperative. How do I tell whether the old pipes are fit for year-round living?

By looking at them where they can be seen: at the supply entry, under the sinks, by the water heater and in the basement or crawl space. Pipes laid for summer may be thinner, with many joints, and run through unheated places. If there are many such sections, it is usually better to replace the whole water supply than to fix joint by joint.

Krustkalni is in the "Ķekavas nami" tariff zone. Does that mean my house is connected to the network?

No. The tariff zone shows where “Ķekavas nami” provides services, but connection is each house’s own matter. If you have no contract with “Ķekavas nami” and no meter, the house runs on its own system, and a septic tank or holding tank that is not connected to the centralised sewer has to be registered with Ķekava Municipality.
