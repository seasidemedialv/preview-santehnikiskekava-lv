Pipe repair in Ķekava and the surrounding area

Problems with the pipe system are among the most common plumbing emergencies in flats and private houses. A water leak, even one that seems insignificant, can within a few weeks cause mould in the walls, damage to the floor and extra repair costs. That is why it is important to repair pipes in good time.

We repair various pipe systems - both plastic (PVC, PP) and metal (copper, steel, cast iron) pipes.

What do we do in pipe repair?

  • Stopping pipe leaks

    Locating and sealing a water leak - a lasting solution.

  • Replacing worn metal pipes

    We replace old metal pipes with plastic or copper pipes.

  • Clearing blockages

    Removing limescale deposits, rust and other blockages in the pipe system.

  • Water pipe installation

    Laying new water pipes in kitchens, bathrooms and other rooms.

  • Riser repair

    Repair and replacement of the shared water supply risers in blocks of flats.

  • Diagnostics

    Pressure testing and finding the leak - so that the problem is found before it becomes big.

A pressure pump on the supply line: Ķekavas nami permission first

If the water pressure in a house seems too weak, the idea of a pump on the supply line can come up. Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022 (point 22) state that a customer may install a pressure-boosting pump at their own expense only with the service provider’s prior written permission. This applies to houses that receive water from the Ķekavas nami network.

So before a pump it is worth working out where the pressure is lost: whether it is weak already at the supply line or only at a few taps. If everything is in order at the supply line but one branch gives a thin stream, the job is in the house’s pipes or a valve, and it is done with the ordinary pipe repair lines.

Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022, point 22; text checked on likumi.lv on 24 November 2026.

A ball valve on a thick pipe and a pressure reserve

LBN 221-15 (point 123) states that valves that close quickly, for example a ball valve or a cone tap, must not have a nominal diameter larger than 50 mm. For example, the Swedish building rules (Boverket, BFS 2024:8) require the tap water installation to withstand a static pressure of at least 1 MPa (10 bar) and to be designed with hydraulic shocks in mind. This is not a Latvian requirement, but a good measure when choosing pipes and joints for a replacement.

Questions: Pipe repair in Ķekava

Can a ball valve be fitted on a private house's supply line?

LBN 221-15 (point 123) limits quick-closing valves to a nominal diameter of 50 mm. A private house’s supply line is usually much thinner, so a ball valve is allowed there. It just should not be shut with a sudden jerk.

Does a dishwasher need a separate pressure reducer?

Latvian regulations do not require it directly. For example, the Swedish building rules (Boverket) provide that if an appliance cannot withstand 1 MPa (10 bar), such as a dishwasher, pressure reduction must be provided for it. What pressure a particular machine withstands is written in its manual.

In Valdlauči the tap water suddenly smells of chlorine. Are the pipes to blame?

Not necessarily. On 30 October 2026 Ķekavas nami announced that during the preventive disinfection of the networks organised by SIA Rīgas ūdens, from 2 to 6 November 2026, changes in the smell and taste of drinking water are possible in Valdlauči, Krustkalni, Ziedonis and Rāmava. That is a matter of the network, not of the house’s pipes. If the smell stays longer or appears only in the hot water, the cause can be in the house.
