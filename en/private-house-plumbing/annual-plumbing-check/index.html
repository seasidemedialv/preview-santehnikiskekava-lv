Plumbing maintenance in a private house - what the Ķekava regulations require of the owner

In Ķekava Municipality, part of this list is set by the water management binding regulations No. 21/2022 themselves, because they make the owner maintain their own network up to the responsibility boundary and answer for what happens when shut-off valves and non-return valves are missing. In a maintenance visit these points are looked at one by one.

The points of the regulations and what they mean in a maintenance visit

  • Point 38: the owner maintains their own networks, protects the pipes and meter from flooding and frost, keeps the seals, fits automatic shut-offs for basement fixtures and cleans their sewer as needed. In a maintenance visit this means looking at the meter assembly, the basement shut-offs and whether the cleaning points can be reached.
  • Point 56: if the system has no shut-off valves, closed inspection points or non-return valves, the consequences are the user’s responsibility. In a maintenance visit each valve is checked for whether it closes and can be reached, and each inspection point for whether it is behind tiles with no hatch.
  • Points 36 and 37: the owner must keep as-built drawings of the underground and internal water and sewer pipes with diameters, depth, fittings and the meter assembly position; if there are none, survey drawings must be prepared. In a maintenance visit you can note down where the pipes run and where the shut-offs are.
  • Points 39 and 40: the networks on the plot, including the meter assembly, control manholes and pumps, are maintained and repaired at the owner’s expense, and are prepared for winter.

Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022, points 36-40 and 56; the version on likumi.lv was viewed on 24 November 2026. The regulations apply to properties connected to the centralised water supply or sewerage.

The meter assembly: temperature, seals and 24 hours

The general terms of the contract, attached to regulations No. 21/2022 as annex 3, require the customer to protect the meter assembly and the meter: to ensure waterproofing, a temperature not lower than +5 °C, and to report a meter fault within 24 hours. A maintenance visit therefore looks not only at the pipes by the meter but at the room or manhole itself: whether it is damp, whether the insulation is in place and whether the seals are intact.

If the meter assembly is in a manhole in the yard, an autumn maintenance visit is the time to check the manhole cover and insulation, because points 39 and 40 make the owner prepare their own networks and the meter assembly for winter.

Regulations No. 21/2022, annex 3 (general terms of the contract), point 4.2, and points 30, 39 and 40; likumi.lv, checked on 24 November 2026.

If the house has a septic tank or a treatment plant

Houses without a connection to the centralised sewerage in Ķekava Municipality have rules of their own, binding regulations No. 20/2022. A septic tank must be registered in the municipal register, and for removing its sludge a contract or receipt must be kept, showing the address, quantity, date and the carrier’s details. For a biological treatment plant, the owner must submit once a year, by 1 June, a confirmation from a competent company on the plant’s technical maintenance.

The register also matters when something changes in the house. If the system is rebuilt, the house is connected to the centralised sewerage or the number of residents changes, the owner must tell the municipality no later than within one month. A plumbing maintenance visit can check the house side: the outlet to the fixture, the inspection points and whether the waste pipe runs freely.

The check point by point and the separate prices

There is no single price for a yearly plumbing check. It is set by the size of the house and the number of fixtures, whether the water heater and a sewage pump have to be checked, and whether the small faults found are fixed in the same visit. Individual rows have starting prices: routine water-heater service with a check of scale, anode and safety valve from €75, clearing a drain with a cable from €40.

How often a water heater needs servicing also depends on the water that gets into it; there is a page about this, water heater servicing and descaling. If the house is left unheated in winter, a maintenance visit is also the time to decide about winter draining.

Questions: Plumbing maintenance in a private house

The house has no pipe diagram at all. Is that a problem?

Points 36 and 37 of the Ķekava regulations require the as-built drawings of underground and internal pipes to be kept, and if there are none, survey drawings must be prepared. In daily life, a missing diagram means that in an emergency nobody knows where the pipe runs and where the shut-off is. A maintenance visit can at least note down the positions of the valves and inspection points.

The meter in the basement is damaged. Who pays for it?

Point 30 of regulations No. 21/2022 provides that the customer pays for a stolen or damaged meter, unless the meter assembly is outside the customer’s responsibility boundary. The contract terms require a fault to be reported to SIA “Ķekavas nami” within 24 hours.

More people live in the house now than when we registered the septic tank. Does anything have to be done?

Yes. The Ķekava regulations on decentralised sewage systems require changes, including a different number of residents, to be reported to the municipality within one month. For a holding tank, the minimum emptying frequency also depends on the number of residents.
