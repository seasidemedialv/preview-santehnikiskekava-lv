Garden house water supply and sewer for year-round living - when a summer house becomes a home

A garden house water supply that was meant for summer becomes the weakest point of the house in winter once people live there permanently.

In Mellupi, some summer houses have been converted into detached houses, while in Jenči and Dzērumi detached houses have already been built alongside summer houses (village assessment of 2019). The water route has to be rebuilt together with the house: the service pipe in the ground, the pipes under the floor, the outdoor tap and the internal waste.

The service pipe in the ground: deeper or warmer

A summer water pipe can be buried as shallow as was convenient, because it was drained in winter anyway. For a permanent house, the service pipe has to be below frost depth or be insulated. For example, the Swedish building authority Boverket writes that pipes laid in the ground must be at a sufficient depth or insulated.

Moving the service pipe deeper is not always possible: there are trees on the way, a neighbour’s fence or a street. The streets of the cooperatives belong to different owners. In Dzērumi, most of the internal streets of the garden cooperatives belong to the societies themselves, while in Mellupi and Jenči the street network has passed into municipal ownership (assessment of 2019). This decides whom you agree the digging with on the street side. If it is not possible to dig deeper, the service pipe is insulated and a heating cable is fitted.

A cable inside the pipe or on the pipe

A heating cable can be pulled inside a water pipe or run along the outside of the pipe. Inside, it can be pulled into an existing service pipe without digging, but outside it is fitted when the pipe is exposed anyway or when a sewer has to be heated.

Nexans
for sewer pipes the cable may be fitted only on the outside; inside a water pipe, up to 60 m of DEFROST WATER cable can be pulled in. A thermostat with an external sensor is recommended, often in direct contact with the cable, so that the cable does not overheat.
Ensto Plug’n Heat (EFPPH)
a self-regulating cable of about 9 W/m at 10 °C, so 10 m is about 90 W. In a water pipe it is fed in through a pressure gland, but the joint with the supply cable must not be pushed into the gland; the smallest bend radius is 40 mm.
Electricity
in Ensto’s Finnish instructions, a 30 mA residual-current device must be used with the Plug’n Heat cable. Connecting the cable to the mains is an electrician’s job.

These figures apply to specific models. Before choosing a cable you need to know the length of the service pipe from the manhole or connection point to the house and the pipe diameter, because they decide whether a cable can be pulled in at all.

Pipes in the house: on the warm side

In a house built for summer, pipes may run through a ventilated underfloor void, along an outside wall or in the attic. If the house is now heated, the rooms are warm, but the underfloor void in winter is as cold as outdoors.

For example, the Swedish industry rules Säker Vatteninstallation 2026:1 prohibit pipes in unheated spaces where there is a risk of freezing: crawl spaces, attics and the building’s thermal insulation in floors, outside walls or attic ceilings. They are placed on the warm side of the structure, inside the vapour barrier, and service pipes in unheated spaces are protected against frost. In a rebuild this means moving pipes from the underfloor void to the room side, for example along an inner wall or in the floor above the insulation.

The outdoor tap and the internal waste

An ordinary tap on an outside wall stays full of water in winter. Säker Vatten 2026:1 requires an outdoor tap to have a drain that empties it when the tap is closed, even if a hose is connected. The choice of tap and the hole in the wall depend on the thickness and material of the wall.

If the house has so far had only a kitchen sink, permanent living needs a bathroom with a WC, a shower and a water heater, and all of them need a waste pipe to a septic tank, a holding tank or a connection manhole. If the house is not connected to the centralised sewerage, its decentralised system must be registered in the Ķekava Municipality register.

The price of rebuilding a garden house

There is no single price for rebuilding a garden house. The sum is set by the depth and length of the service pipe, whether a heating cable and insulation are needed, and how much internal waste pipe and how many fixtures have to be built. An outdoor tap with a winter shut-off and thawing a frozen service pipe are priced separately. A run to one fixture costs from €35.

What people ask before sending a request

Can a cable be pulled into the existing service pipe without digging it up?

Yes, if the service pipe is not too long and has no sharp bends. For the Nexans DEFROST WATER cable the manufacturer gives up to 60 m inside the pipe, and for the Ensto Plug’n Heat cable a smallest bend radius of 40 mm. The cable is fed in through a pressure gland on the house side.

A sewer pipe leaves the garden house and freezes in winter. Can it be heated from the inside?

No. The manufacturer Nexans states that for sewer pipes a cable may be fitted only on the outside of the pipe. So the pipe is dug up, a cable is placed on it and insulated, or the pipe is moved deeper.

We heat the house in winter. Is that not enough to keep the pipes from freezing?

It is enough for the rooms, but not always for a pipe in a ventilated underfloor void or in the outside wall insulation. These are exactly the places that the Swedish Säker Vatten rules prohibit for water pipes. If a pipe runs through them, it is moved to the warm side of the structure or protected against frost.
