Winterising a summer house water system - when the house is left unheated

A summer house water system has to be winterised before frost reaches an unheated house: the water must be let out of the pipes, the water heater and the appliances, and the heating loop must be empty or filled with a liquid that does not freeze.

In the Ķekava area this applies to houses in former garden societies that are still used only in summer or at weekends. Home policy terms also ask for it, and each company has its own threshold and its own idea of when a house counts as lived in.

The policy threshold: 0 °C, 1 °C or a house with no permanent residents

If a pipe bursts in an unheated house in winter, the policy terms decide whether the loss will be compensated. The terms of three Latvian companies put it differently:

Terms When the water must be let out What must be done
BALTA, No. 1202.09, 8.1. g) outdoor temperature below 0 °C and the property is not heated empty the water supply, plumbing and heating systems of water
If, No. TPI-S-20243-LV, point 72.8 the property is not heated and the air temperature is below 1 °C drain the water from the heating and water systems
ERGO Premium, IP 1 December 2025, points 7.1, 7.2, 7.14 the property is not permanently inhabited or part of it is not heated drain the water, shut off the sewer and heating lines, supervise the water pipes; if this is not done, compensation may be reduced by up to 50% or refused

Terms checked on 24 November 2026. These are examples: your policy may have a different threshold, so read the safety requirements of your own policy before winter.

The 21 days in the If terms: a garden house visited at weekends

The policy requirements for an unheated house also apply to a house that someone visits at weekends. For example, in the If terms a home counts as not permanently inhabited if it is lived in only seasonally, or if it is left without supervision for more than 21 days in a row more often than once a year. Supervision means inspecting the rooms both inside and outside, not just glancing at the windows while driving past.

So a garden house that is left without inspection several times in winter for more than three weeks is, under these terms, not permanently inhabited, even if someone sometimes visits. Then letting the water out and preparing the heating loop must be done before the first frost, not after the next visit.

If home policy terms No. TPI-S-20243-LV (in force from 24 September 2024), checked on 24 November 2026. This is an example: the definition in your policy may be different.

What the Ķekava regulations require of the owner

Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022 make the owner prepare their own pipes and meter assembly for winter. If the meter is in the house, the contract terms require the assembly to be kept at a temperature not lower than +5 °C. Leaving water running before the meter so that the service pipe does not freeze is prohibited unless this has been agreed with the service provider (point 54.6). The seals on the meter must be kept intact, so do not take the assembly apart yourself.

Mixer taps, the WC cistern and machines

When the pipes are empty, water still remains in the appliances. The manufacturer FM Mattsson says of its mixer taps: if the tap will be at a temperature below 0 °C, for example in an unheated summer house, the water pipes and the tap must be drained and the tap left open or removed and kept in a warm room. The same applies to a shower mixer.

The WC cistern and the hoses of the washing machine and dishwasher are drained separately. The ERGO Premium terms for an unheated house also require the sewer lines to be shut off, so for winter the drains are closed too, where there is a plug or valve for it.

The heating loop: empty or with antifreeze

If the house has radiator heating that is not used in winter, the loop can be drained or filled with antifreeze made for heating systems.

For example, the manufacturer Fernox says of its Alphi-11 (a monopropylene glycol antifreeze with an inhibitor): a 25% concentration protects down to -11 °C, 30% to -15 °C, 35% to -18 °C and 40% to -22 °C. The smallest recommended concentration for corrosion protection is 25%, and it must be checked every year. Antifreeze reduces the heat output of the radiators; read more on this under radiator not heating.

The scope of winterising: points, water heaters and the heating loop

There is no single price for autumn draining and spring start-up. It is set by the number of water points and water heaters, whether antifreeze has to go into the heating loop, and whether the service pipe has a shut-off in the basement or a manhole. If in spring the water heater also needs servicing, routine water-heater service costs from €75.

Questions: Winterising a summer house water system

The loop has 30% antifreeze. Is that enough for a Ķekava winter?

The manufacturer Fernox says of its Alphi-11 that a 30% concentration protects down to -15 °C, 35% to -18 °C and 40% to -22 °C. Which limit to choose depends on how cold the house can get; the concentration is checked every year, because below 25% the antifreeze no longer protects against corrosion.

The meter is in the house, not in a manhole. What should be done with it if we do not heat the house?

The contract terms require not less than +5 °C in the assembly, and the seals must not be touched by you. If you will not heat the room in winter, agree with SIA “Ķekavas nami” on shutting off the service pipe and protecting the meter before the frost starts.
