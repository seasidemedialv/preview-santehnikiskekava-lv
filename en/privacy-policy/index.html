Privacy policy

This page describes what happens to the data you enter in the request form on santehnikiskekava.lv.

What data comes from the form

The fields you fill in: name, phone number, place (district), address, type of problem, urgency and, if you enter one, a description. Sent along with them are the page the request was sent from, the time it was sent and a technical number that stops one request from being received twice.

The site collects no other information about your visit: it has no analytics, advertising pixels or cookies.

For what purpose

Only to contact you about the job you requested and to carry it out. Once you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. The data is processed because you yourself ask us to get in touch and to do the work (point (b) of Article 6(1) of the General Data Protection Regulation). We do not sell the data and do not use it for advertising.

Who processes the data

  • Cloudflare (site hosting) keeps a copy of the request so that it is not lost if there is a communication failure; the copy is deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Activepieces (request handling) passes the request to a specialist and records it in the request list.
  • A WhatsApp message saying that the request has been received is sent to the phone number given in the form.

These service providers use the data only for this purpose.

Retention period

Copy in the site’s storage: 90 days. In the request list and in messages: for as long as it is needed to carry out the job and to answer questions about it, unless you ask for deletion earlier.

Your rights

You can find out what data of yours has been received, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to its processing. You can send the request through the same form: write what you want in the description field. A complaint can be lodged with the Data State Inspectorate (dvi.gov.lv).

Fonts, scripts and images

The site does not load fonts, scripts or images from other companies' servers, so when you open a page your IP address is not passed to such services.

If the policy changes

The new version is published on this same page. Updated on 24 November 2026.
