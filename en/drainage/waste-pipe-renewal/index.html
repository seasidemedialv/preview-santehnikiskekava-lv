If the sewer leaks or a pipe has cracked, replacing the horizontal waste pipe to one fixture costs from €30, and a soil stack stretch in a flat from €100 per storey. For a yard pipe the damaged spot is found first, because it decides how much has to be dug.

A grey pipe in the yard: when asbestos rules apply to the work

An old yard sewer can be made of ceramic or fibre-cement pipes, and fibre cement may contain asbestos. In that case dismantling falls under Cabinet Regulation No. 852 on occupational safety requirements for work with asbestos.

The regulation requires the employer to submit an application to the State Labour Inspectorate 10 working days before work with asbestos; dismantling may start with the inspectorate’s permission, and if no refusal is issued within 10 working days, the permission is considered given. An application is not needed only when all three conditions are met together: the exposure is sporadic and of low intensity, the risk assessment shows that the limit value will not be exceeded, and the work is, for example, short maintenance with non-friable materials or removal of undamaged material in which the fibres are firmly bound.

Products containing asbestos are removed before the installation is dismantled, and the waste is collected, at the latest at the end of the work stage, in closed packaging marked as containing asbestos. When planning the replacement of a yard pipe, this application period must be counted into the work schedule before a trench is dug.

Cabinet Regulation No. 852 applies to an employer whose employees work with asbestos. Checked on 24 November 2026 on likumi.lv.

The bed for a new pipe in peaty ground

LBN 223-15 allows reinforced concrete, concrete, ceramic, cast iron, fibre-cement, fibreglass and plastic pipes for external gravity sewers. Pipes are laid straight on a levelled and compacted trench bottom; in rocky ground a 15 cm bedding of sand or gravel is needed, and in silty and peaty ground an artificial base.

Around Ķekava this is not just theory: Baloži was built in a marshy area, part of its territory is taken by the Medema bog, and the shores of Lake Titurga are low and peaty. Without a base, a pipe can sink unevenly in peat, and where a dip forms, sediment starts to collect.

A cast-iron soil stack stretch in a flat and the branches to fixtures

In a block of flats the soil stack serves all the flats on it, so replacing a stretch of it is agreed with the building manager. The line Ø110 soil stack in a flat: cast-iron section replaced with plastic covers a Ø110 stretch in one flat with the transition to the cast-iron stretches next door.

The horizontal waste pipes to a sink, bath or WC are usually part of the flat and can be replaced separately, with a fall from the fixture to the stack. More on the building’s decision and the water risers on the page riser replacement.

A cast-iron soil stack usually starts to leak at the sockets, where the old seal has crumbled, or through a crack along the pipe. Then water runs down the wall only when someone upstairs uses water, and the spot on the ceiling is damp after the evening showers, not after the night.

Waste pipe, soil stack stretch and yard pipe: the price lines

Replacing the horizontal waste pipe to one fixture: from €30 per fixture. It means taking out the old stretch and fitting a new one with a fall to the stack or the horizontal drain; materials extra. Ø110 soil stack in a flat: cast-iron section replaced with plastic: from €100 per storey, agreeing with the building manager and materials extra.

There is no price line for replacing a yard pipe: the work and the price are discussed in conversation, because they depend on length, depth, ground and whether the old pipe is fibre cement. The price of a video check of a drain depends on the length of the drain and access, on whether the drain first has to be flushed, and on whether a recording with the damaged spot is needed; the check lets you dig only where the pipe is damaged.

About old sewer pipes

How can I tell a fibre-cement pipe from a ceramic one once it has been dug up?

A ceramic pipe is usually glazed, brown or reddish brown, with sockets. A fibre-cement pipe is grey and matt, and fibres can be seen at a break. If it is unclear, the material can be tested in a laboratory before the pipe is broken.

A cast-iron soil stack leaks at a socket. Can it just be resealed?

A socket can be resealed if the pipe itself is sound and the seal is the only fault. If the cast iron around the socket has cracked or rusted through, a new seal will not hold, and the stretch is replaced with plastic, agreed with the building manager.

Where does a dug-up asbestos-cement pipe go?

Cabinet Regulation No. 301 allows construction waste containing asbestos to be disposed of only in separate sections of municipal waste landfills or in landfills that accept only such waste. Packing and labelling are the responsibility of the waste holder, and supervision is carried out by the State Environmental Service.

Can the new yard pipe follow the route of the old one?

It can, if the route allows the necessary fall and the ground is suitable. If the old drain has sunk or there is peat on the route, the new drain needs a base or another route. More on the fall on the page drain flushing.
