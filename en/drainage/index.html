Drain problems - a solution in Ķekava

A blocked drain is one of the most unpleasant problems in a home. Water that will not drain away or comes back up, smells from the pipes and overflowing sinks - all of this points to problems in the drainage system that should be dealt with as soon as possible. If a drain is not cleared in good time, there is a risk of damaging the services and causing damage to neighbours.

A drain is cleared mechanically or with high-pressure flushing, depending on the blockage. If the cause is not clear, the location of the blockage can be found with a video inspection before the work starts.

We serve both private houses and blocks of flats in Ķekava and the surrounding area - in Baloži, Katlakalns, Rāmava, Valdlauči, Salaspils and other places. Drain cleaning is available also at night and at weekends, also in emergencies.

A gravity drain up to the boundary, network pumps beyond

In the villages of Ķekava parish, the street network does not carry wastewater to treatment by fall alone. In Ziedonis, in stage 3 of the Cohesion Fund project (2012–2015), besides 8,030 m of gravity sewer, 1,295 m of pressure main and three sewage pumping stations were built, and the wastewater goes on to the pressure main that leads from Ķekava to Riga. In Rāmava the same stage laid 456 m of pressure main and two pumping stations.

The house’s internal drain and the yard drain up to the connection point, by contrast, are gravity drains: they work only with their own fall. So a blockage in the house or yard has to be cleared from the house side, with a cable or flushing. How much fall a yard drain needs is described on the page drain flushing, and what pipes it is relaid with and on what base, on the page sewer pipe replacement.

Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022 (point 49) forbid discharging solids, fats and floating particles larger than 4 mm into the centralised sewer; what this means for a home day to day, including a home with a septic tank or its own pump, is written in the guide water will not drain.

Questions: Drain unblocking in Ķekava

The drain gurgles when I empty the bath. Is that already a blockage?

It can be. For example, the Helsinki water utility HSY names smell, gurgling and a water level that often rises in floor drains and the WC as signs of a blocked pipe. If it gurgles and water drains more slowly in several places, the blockage can be further along in the shared drain, not in a single trap. If only one sink gurgles and the water drains well, see the page sink trap replacement.

Do the Ķekava water management rules also apply to a house with a septic tank?

Binding regulations No. 21/2022 (point 5) do not apply to decentralised sewage, the discharge of rainwater or separate rainwater systems. They apply to houses that send wastewater into the centralised network. A septic tank has its own rules, and it is emptied by sewage-tanker operators, not by a plumber.
