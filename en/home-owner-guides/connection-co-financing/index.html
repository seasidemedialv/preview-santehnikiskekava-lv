Ķekava Municipality co-financing for a connection - how to apply and what you must do yourself

Co-financing for a sewer or water connection in Ķekava Municipality can cover all the costs of the network section up to the connection point on your property, but not more than €2,500 for the connection of a detached or semi-detached house.

It is set by the municipality’s binding regulations No. 14/2023 with amendments No. 3/2026. You can apply only after the municipality publishes a notice, and the house’s internal networks are not part of the co-financing.

The short answer: what to do and when

Follow the notice in the “Ķekavas Novads” publication and on the websites of the municipality and its municipal companies, prepare a land boundary plan showing the existing systems and the desired connection point, and submit the application within 60 days of publication. When the municipality makes its decision, you conclude a three-party contract with the municipality and SIA “Ķekavas nami” within 10 working days of the invitation. In the meantime you can plan your own part: the service pipe from the connection point to the house and the switch-over inside the house.

Whether the house and you meet the rules

Property
in Ķekava Municipality, with a residential house registered in the cadastre, and a centralised water supply and/or sewer has already been built along the street or road by the property.
Applicant
a natural person whose ownership rights are entered in the land register and whose place of residence was declared in Ķekava Municipality on 1 January of the current year; for a block of flats, the manager with the authorisation of the owners' general meeting.
Debts and violations
support is not granted if building rule violations have been found at the person’s property in the municipality, and since 6 March 2026 also if there are debts to the municipality for real estate tax or to the municipality’s companies for services.
How often
co-financing is granted to one property only once; in co-ownership the amount is calculated in proportion to the shares.

The order of applying

  1. Notice. The municipality publishes a notice in “Ķekavas Novads” and on its own and its companies' websites; applications are accepted for 60 days from publication.
  2. Application. Fill in the application form (an annex to the regulations), attach documents on the priority group and a land boundary plan marking the existing systems, for example a holding tank, and the desired connection point.
  3. Commission. A three-member commission set up by the executive director examines applications in the order of registration, requests estimates from the municipal company, calculates the amount and makes a decision. If the budget has run out, eligible applicants get priority in the next round.
  4. Contract. The municipality, the private person and the municipal company conclude a three-party contract; the private person must sign it within 10 working days of the invitation. The work is organised by the municipal company, observing free and fair competition.
  5. After the work. A service contract is concluded with the municipal company, and the municipality transfers the co-financing in accordance with the contract. The decision can be cancelled if the contracts are not signed or the applicant refuses.

Priority groups under point 7

The regulations list the groups that have priority. The first are persons with the status of a person in need or on a low income; families with three or more children under 18 (up to 24 if studying), at least three of whom are declared in the municipality. Next come a person with a disability or their carer; a politically repressed person; a Chernobyl nuclear plant accident clean-up participant; an Afghanistan war participant. The list also includes a participant of the National Resistance Movement; a pensioner; one or more children under 18 (up to 24 if studying) who are declared at the property to be connected.

If both a sewer and a water network have been built along the property, priority goes to the sewer connection. The status is proved with copies of documents attached to the application.

The regulations and the source

Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 14/2023 (9 August 2023) “On co-financing from Ķekava Municipality for connecting residential houses to the centralised water supply system and/or the centralised sewer system”, consolidated version incorporating the 2026 amendments No. 3/2026 (in force from 6 March 2026); text on likumi.lv checked on 24 November 2026. The amounts are maximums, and co-financing is granted within the budget of the current year.

The regulations apply only to Ķekava Municipality, including Baloži and Baldone. In Iecava, Bauska Municipality binding regulations No. 4 are in force, which provide co-financing only for a sewer connection: €20 per metre and €100 per inspection chamber, not more than €1,500 per connection.

What the amount covers and what stays with you

Co-financing applies to the connection, that is, the part of the network from the water management service provider’s network to the connection point on the private person’s property. It includes design, if needed, construction work, and the engineering survey and registration of the built services in the municipality’s topographic information database. The amount is set from an estimate prepared by the municipal company. The limit can be raised to €5,000 if the connection can be used by at least two more eligible properties.

The building’s internal water supply and sewer system is not part of the co-financing. What stays with you is the service pipe from the connection point to the house, if it is not in the co-financed section, the meter assembly, switching the house drain from the septic tank to the new outlet, and decommissioning the old septic tank, which point 16 of binding regulations No. 21/2022 requires. The service pipe from the boundary to the house has no single price: it is set by length, depth, crossings and the entry through the foundations.

What people ask before sending a request

We are pensioners, but our residence is declared in Riga. Can we apply?

Under point 6.1 of the regulations, the applicant’s place of residence must be declared in Ķekava Municipality on 1 January of the current year. So you can apply only in a year when the declaration in Ķekava Municipality was already in place on 1 January.

Will the municipality also pay for decommissioning the septic tank and switching over inside the house?

No. The regulations expressly exclude the building’s internal water supply and sewer system, and under binding regulations No. 21/2022 decommissioning the old septic tank is the owner’s duty. The co-financing covers only the network part up to the connection point.

May we order the co-financed section from our own builder?

The co-financed section is organised by the municipal company under the three-party contract, observing free and fair competition. Your own part beyond the connection point and the work inside the house you order from whoever you wish.

We missed the notice. When is the next chance?

The regulations set no dates: the municipality announces applications within the budget of the current year and publishes a notice. Follow the “Ķekavas Novads” publication and the websites kekava.lv and kekavasnami.lv.
