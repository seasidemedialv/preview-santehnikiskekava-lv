Radiator not heating - in a house where the owner looks after the heating circuit

A radiator does not heat when not enough warm water reaches it or when it cannot give its heat to the room.

If a radiator in a house with its own heating still heats poorly after bleeding, check how it is connected, what covers it and what is in the heating circuit. For example, the manufacturer KORAD states that a steel panel radiator connected the wrong way round loses 30–40% of its heat output.

Flow at the top, return at the bottom

KORAD calls a standard connection one from a single side, where the warm water flow enters at the top and the return leaves at the bottom. If the pipes are swapped, the radiator loses 30–40% of its heat output and the thermostatic valve may become noisy.

This can happen after a radiator is replaced, moved to another wall or after the heating pipework in the house has been rebuilt. So first compare: is one radiator weak or all of them. If the weak one is exactly the one that was recently replaced or moved, check which pipe is its flow.

Curtains, windowsill and laundry on the radiator

A radiator heats a room with the air that flows past it. If a thick curtain hangs in front or the radiator is squeezed under a low windowsill, the heat stays behind the curtain or by the window. How much space a radiator needs above and below it is described on the page about radiator replacement.

KORAD also advises not to use a radiator for drying clothes: the heat output then falls by about half and the risk of corrosion grows. A room where laundry dries on the radiator all winter feels cold, although the radiator itself is hot.

Antifreeze in the circuit: heat that stays in the liquid

If a house in one of the former garden societies around Ķekava used to stand unheated in winter, its heating circuit may contain antifreeze. KORAD states that antifreeze reduces the heat output of radiators:

20% mixture
freezes at -10 °C, heat output 5% lower.
35% mixture
freezes at -20 °C, heat output 10% lower.
45% mixture
freezes at -30 °C, heat output 15% lower.

An antifreeze made for heating must be used, not car coolant. The manufacturer DAB allows glycol of no more than 30% for its Evosta 2/3 circulation pumps. Look in the house’s papers or on the cans for a note of what was poured into the circuit and in what concentration. If the house is now heated all winter, you can consider flushing the circuit and filling it with water, but then the house can no longer be left unheated without draining it.

The pump by the heating boiler: shaft, pressure and bleeding

The DAB Evosta 2/3 manual requires the pump motor shaft to be horizontal, shut-off valves on both sides, the system to be flushed before installation and the minimum working pressure to be 1 bar. The electronics must never be insulated. The automatic bleeding of the Evosta 3 starts when the button is held for 3 seconds, and the pump then runs at maximum speed for a minute.

Before changing anything, note down the pressure that the gauge by the heating boiler shows in the cold system and compare it with the pump and heating boiler manuals; DAB requires at least 1 bar for these pumps. If the gauge shows less, radiators on the upper floor may stay cold, and then you have to look for where the circuit loses pressure.

When the repair is no longer about one radiator

If all the radiators are cold after the heating boiler or another heat source in the house was replaced, the question is about the whole circuit: valves, balancing and temperature. For this case the building code LBN 231-15 has its own point, and it is described on the page about heating system repair.

Fixing a radiator valve, thermostatic head or leak, and bleeding the circuit with a pressure top-up, have no single price. It is set by the location of the fault, whether the system has to be drained, the number of radiators and whether air returns and the cause has to be found, for example in the expansion vessel. Questions: Radiator not heating

A radiator was moved to another window, and now it heats more weakly. Why?

Two things need checking first: whether the flow and return have been swapped (see the section above on the connection) and whether in the new place the radiator is covered by a windowsill or a curtain.

May car coolant be poured into a heating system?

No. KORAD states that an antifreeze made for heating must be used, not car coolant. Heating antifreeze also reduces heat output, so the concentration is chosen only as high as needed, not the maximum.

In autumn we poured antifreeze into the circuit, and now all the radiators heat more weakly. Is that normal?

In part, yes. KORAD states that a 20% mixture reduces heat output by 5%, 35% by 10% and 45% by 15%. If the drop seems bigger, check the concentration and the pressure in the circuit, because antifreeze alone does not make a radiator cold.
