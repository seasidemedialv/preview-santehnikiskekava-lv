What to do in the first minutes after an emergency

If you have an urgent plumbing problem, act quickly to limit the damage

  • 1

    Shut the main water valve

    It is usually next to the water meter or in the basement. It stops the flow of water and prevents further damage.

  • 2

    Switch off the electricity in wet areas

    If you can do it safely and without standing in water, switch off the electricity in the rooms where water is leaking, to avoid the risk of electric shock.

  • 3

    Collect the spilled water

    Use buckets, towels or mops to collect the water and protect the floor and furniture from damage.

    Request the job in the form

Ķekava tap water and limescale in a water heater

In an analysis by SIA “Ķekavas nami” on 6 May 2026 at the “Dālderi” shopping centre on Nākotnes iela, the total hardness of the Ķekava network water was 3.02 mmol/L and iron was below 0.04 mg/L. There is practically no iron in this water, but there are enough calcium and magnesium salts to settle out when heated.

In an electric water heater they can collect on the heating element and the tank floor, in a mixer tap on the aerator screen and in the cartridge, and in a WC cistern on the fill valve. Water heater descaling (from €75) removes these deposits: the heater is drained, the deposits are taken out, and the anode and safety valve are checked. A dripping tap can be fixed by replacing the washer or cartridge (from €25).

Where Ķekava's sewage goes

Ķekava’s wastewater is not treated in Ķekava itself. Under the project “Expansion and reconstruction of sewer networks in Ķekava”, a 12 km pressure main with pumping stations to Riga was built by early 2009, and from there the wastewater is treated at the Riga “Daugavgrīva” biological treatment plant.

For a house this means that everything that goes into the sink or the WC then travels on through pumps. Wet wipes and congealed fat first block the house’s own pipe: the internal drain is cleared with a cable (from €40), and a long yard pipe to the manhole is flushed with a high-pressure jet (from €80 with the first hour of work). More on this on the page drain cleaning.

What people ask before calling in Ķekava

What does the price of plumbing work depend on?

Prices depend on how complex the job is. For example, mixer tap repair starts from €25 and pipe repair from €35. You discuss the work and the price when you talk to the plumber.

Can I book a plumber at weekends and on public holidays?

Yes, in emergencies we work also at night and at weekends.

Does a plumber's visit have to be arranged in advance?

In an emergency no advance booking is needed: fill in the form and we contact you within 5 minutes.

What plumbing troubles can you help with?

We deal with a range of plumbing problems: pipe leaks and repairs, drain blockages and cleaning, mixer tap fitting and repair, servicing of water heaters, WC and sink installation, and replacing the plumbing in a bathroom.

What does the length of a plumbing repair depend on?

It depends on how complex the problem is. A simple mixer tap repair is usually a short job, a pipe repair can take longer, and bigger bathroom jobs may take several days. You discuss the time when you talk to the plumber.

Ķekava has been a town since 2022. Did anything change for plumbing work?

Ķekava received town rights on 1 July 2022. The rules for water supply and sewerage in the municipality, including the town, are set by Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022, and in the town the networks are run by SIA “Ķekavas nami”. For work inside a house, the status changes nothing.

Are all the prices listed in one place?

Yes, the page what it costs has every price line with what each one covers. These are labour prices with “from”; materials and fixtures are extra, and for lines without a figure the price is given after assessing the amount of work.
