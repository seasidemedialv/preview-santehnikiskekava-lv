A 56-flat block in Ķekava: networks replaced from the building's reserve

In 2026, SIA “Ķekavas nami” replaced the cold and hot water pipelines and the circulation and sewer risers in one 56-flat block in Ķekava. The work lasted from July to 1 November and cost more than 92 thousand euros, paid from the building’s reserve.

A full network replacement like this is a decision for the flat owners and a long job. Between such projects the building is left with smaller common-property jobs: replacing one riser section in a flat where it has started to leak, a blocked horizontal pipe in the basement, or a leak in a shared space. These are jobs that the building manager can request separately, once access has been agreed with the flat owners.

Co-financing for a connection of a block of flats

In Ķekava Municipality, a block of flats that is not yet connected to the centralised water supply or sewerage can be put forward for municipal co-financing by the building manager too, if the manager runs the building under a contract and the flat owners' general meeting has authorised it (regulations 14/2023, point 6.2). For a building with at least three flats, the co-financing ceiling is €5,000 (point 10). The procedure is described in the article on co-financing for a connection.

Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 14/2023 (as amended by regulations No. 3/2026), points 6.2 and 10; checked on 24 November 2026.

Prices for common-property jobs: per storey, per call-out, per inspection

The starting prices are per job; the sum is affected by the number of storeys where sections have to be replaced, access to the riser behind boxing or tiles, and materials. Each storey is a row of its own; the total is also affected by the transitions to neighbouring sections and by how many times the riser has to be shut off.

Building managers' questions

The water company changed for Baloži buildings. What changes in the plumbing work?

From 1 September 2026, water supply and sewerage services in Baloži are provided by SIA “Ķekavas nami”, and the reorganisation was completed on 29 October 2026. It does not affect work inside the building, on risers or on basement horizontal pipes; only the company providing the water and sewer services changes.

A horizontal pipe in the basement of a Salaspils building is blocked. Can it be flushed without opening the floor?

Usually yes, if the horizontal pipe has an inspection point or can be reached from a manhole in the yard: high-pressure flushing starts from €80, first hour of work included. If the blockage recurs, the cause can be found with a video check of the sewer, priced by the pipe length and access.

Our building is in Olaine. Do the Ķekava regulations apply to it too?

No. Olaine is the centre of Olaine Municipality, and the networks there are run by AS “Olaines ūdens un siltums” under Olaine Municipality’s rules; Ķekava Municipality’s co-financing does not apply to Olaine. The list of work inside the building is, however, the same, see the Olaine page.

A shared space is damp but it is unclear where from. Can I request just an inspection?

Yes. The row Visit on site and assessing the fault (from €35) is meant for exactly the case when it is not yet clear what is damaged: on a working day the fault is inspected and the price of the work is named, but the repair itself is not included.

Can a loan be used for replacing the risers of the whole building?

In the programme under Cabinet Regulation No. 481 for blocks of flats, where loans are issued by Altum, the supported works include renewing the building’s engineering systems, and the application needs a decision of the flat owners. More on the page riser pipe replacement in a flat.
