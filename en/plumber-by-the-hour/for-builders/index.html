Water on the building site before the house is accepted

In a new build, water is needed long before anyone lives there: for filling the system, testing the heating loop and tiling work. Point 21 of the Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022 says that water from the new connection may be used before the building is accepted for use only with the written consent of the water management service provider and through a meter it has installed.

On sites in Ķekava, Baloži and Baldone this provider is SIA “Ķekavas nami”. For the builder this means the consent and the meter must be written into the schedule before the plumbing first needs water from the network, not after it.

Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022, points 20 and 21; checked on 24 November 2026.

Yard networks for terraced houses stay with the owners

The same regulations (point 20) say that the service provider does not take over the internal networks of buildings, pressure-boosting stations built into houses, or the networks, treatment plants and pumping stations of private houses and terraced houses. In a terraced-house project, the yard water main and sewer up to the connection point therefore remain the responsibility of the buyers or their joint ownership.

That is why on such a site it is worth agreeing at the start where each section will have its own shut-off valve and inspection point, so that a fault in one house does not cut off the water for the neighbours. The pipe route and the entries through the foundations are set by the design.

The meter assembly in the new house

Under point 29 of regulations 21/2022, the diameter of the meter is set by the technical conditions and by the house’s current or planned average consumption. For a terraced house or a house with several bathrooms it is worth knowing this before the meter assembly is built and the shut-off valves and fittings next to it are chosen.

The meter is then installed and sealed by the service provider, provided the assembly was built to the technical conditions (point 17). The plumber’s part is the assembly with shut-off valves before and after the meter, and the pipe from it to the house manifold.

At which stage of the build you need a plumber

The first time is when the walls and the base are ready but the floor covering and wall finish are not yet there: that is when the water and waste outlets are run to every fixture position, as described on the page first-fix plumbing in a private house. If the house will have water underfloor heating, its loops are laid out before the levelling layer, see water underfloor heating installation.

The second time is after the finishes: then the WCs, basins, showers, water heater and manifold are connected. For houses sold in shell finish, the buyer can order this part directly, and it has its own page finishing plumbing in a new build. Streets of new detached houses are, for example, in Krogsils, Jaunsils and Saulgoži.

Rough-in by connection point and finishing: the price lines

Rough-in in a new house starts from €55 for each connection point: a cold or hot water or waste outlet to a fixture from the manifold or riser. For finishing on existing outlets, for installing heating pipes and radiators, and for underfloor heating, the price is worked out from the amount of work: from the number of fixtures and loops, from whether the outlets match the plan, and from the position of the heating boiler or heat pump. Materials are not included in the price.

Questions from the building site

Will Ķekavas nami take over the yard networks of terraced houses after handover?

Under point 20 of Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022, the service provider does not take over the networks of private houses and terraced houses. They remain with the owners, who are responsible for them.

Who installs the water meter in the new house?

For a house connected to the SIA “Ķekavas nami” network, a tested meter is installed and sealed by the service provider in a meter assembly that the owner builds, and according to the company’s information the meter is paid for by SIA “Ķekavas nami”. The plumber prepares the assembly with shut-off valves and does not fit the meter.

The house was sold in shell finish. What does the buyer still have to do?

Usually all the fixtures have to be connected to the existing outlets, along with the water heater and the underfloor heating or radiator manifold. It is worth first checking that the outlets match the plan and have been pressure-tested; this is described on the page finishing plumbing in a new build.
