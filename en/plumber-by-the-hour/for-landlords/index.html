The meter in a rented house: what the Ķekava regulations require

Point 30 of Ķekava Municipality binding regulations No. 21/2022 provides that the user pays for a stolen or damaged water meter, unless the meter assembly is outside the user’s responsibility boundary. The general terms of the contract (annex 3, point 4.2) require the meter assembly to be protected so that the temperature in it is not lower than +5 °C, and a meter fault to be reported within 24 hours.

If the contract with the water company was signed by the owner, these duties stay with the owner, even when a tenant lives in the house. It therefore helps to agree in the tenancy contract that the tenant reports a burst, a freeze or a meter fault at once, not at the next meeting; otherwise the 24-hour period can pass before the landlord even learns about the fault.

When a tenant reports a fault

If the tenant reports a single fixture, the job usually has its own price line. A dripping tap is usually fixed by replacing the washer or cartridge (from €25); a WC cistern that keeps running gets a new fill valve or flush mechanism (from €35); a blocked sink or shower is cleared with a cable (from €40). If water is already running into the neighbours' flat, request the emergency service, which can also be called out at night and at weekends.

A vacant home between tenants, and the policy

When one tenant moves out and the next has not yet been found, a home can stand empty for weeks, and that can matter for the policy. For example, in the terms of If, a home that is left without supervision for more than 21 days in a row more often than once a year may be treated as not permanently inhabited. Between tenants it is worth agreeing who inspects the home; more about policy conditions for an unheated house is on the page winter draining.

An unheated rental house in winter

If a rented home stands empty for a while in winter, the pipes and the meter assembly still have to be protected from frost, and this duty stays with the owner. In a flat in a block of flats this is usually provided by the building’s heating; in a private house or garden house that is not heated, the water system is drained in autumn and started again in spring.

How this is done and what to bear in mind with a water heater and a heating circuit is written on the page winter draining; if a pipe has frozen after all, there is the page frozen pipes.

Price lines for a rented flat or house

The prices are the same as for any flat or house: per job, with “from”, parts extra. The final sum is affected by the amount of work, access and materials; when several small jobs are done in one visit, they can be charged by the plumber’s hour.

Landlords' questions

The tenant says the meter has frozen. What should be done on the first day?

For a meter fault, the contract terms require reporting to the water company within 24 hours. The pipes before and after the meter are inspected and thawed by the plumber, and there is a line priced by the amount of work, described on the page frozen pipes. Replacing the meter is arranged by the water company, and under point 30 of the regulations the user pays for a damaged meter if the assembly is within the user’s responsibility boundary.

In a rented flat in Baloži the WC runs all the time. What usually has to be replaced?

For an exposed cistern, usually the fill valve or the flush mechanism; the work starts from €35, parts extra. If the cistern is concealed in the wall, the price depends on whether the fault can be reached through the flush-plate opening, see concealed cistern repair.

The tenant wants to connect their own dishwasher. Is that a small job?

If the kitchen already has a tap and a drain outlet for the machine, the hook-up starts from €35. If there is no outlet, it is built as a new run to one fixture (from €35). More on the page washing machine and dishwasher hook-up.
