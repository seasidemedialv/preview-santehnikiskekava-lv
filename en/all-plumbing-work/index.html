Questions about the range of services

Are the services in Salaspils and Olaine the same as in Ķekava?

The list of work is the same. What differs is the water company and the rules: in Salaspils the networks are run by SIA “Valgums-S”, in Olaine by AS “Olaines ūdens un siltums”, and Ķekava Municipality co-financing does not apply to these towns. More about each place is on the pages Salaspils and Olaine.

The bathroom section has no tiling. What does it include?

The plumbing part: water and waste outlets, the WC, the sink, the bath, the shower cabin and connecting them. Bathroom plumbing from scratch starts from €450 per room; tiling, fixtures and materials are not in that price.

The house is heated by its own heating boiler. Does the heating section apply to it too?

Yes: radiators, water underfloor heating, the circulation pump, the expansion vessel and the heating pipes in a house with its own heating boiler or heat pump are jobs of the heating section. The heating boiler or the heat pump itself is not on this list: the section starts with the pipes and radiators behind it.
