Questions about the price lines

What does the bathroom row “from €450” cover?

It is the labour price for building all the water and waste outlets and fitting the fixtures in one bathroom. Tiling, the fixtures themselves and materials are not in this price. If only two or three fixtures with their runs have to be changed, there is a separate partial-refresh row, priced by the amount of work.

Who pays for the water meter if a house connects to the “Ķekavas nami” network?

The meter is not a plumber’s row. According to SIA “Ķekavas nami”, when a house connects to the network, the company fits and seals the meter before the contract, and SIA “Ķekavas nami” pays for it. The plumber’s work starts after the meter.

Both visit rows have a starting price of €35. How do they differ?

The ordinary visit is on a working day: the plumber looks at the fault and names the price of the work. The emergency call-out is for when the water has to be shut off at once, also at night and at weekends; it covers shutting off the water and isolating the damaged spot, and work at night or at a weekend can cost more. Repair and materials are extra in both cases.
