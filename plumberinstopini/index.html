If you are looking for a plumber in Stopiņi, your house is most likely in one of the parish’s villages: Saurieši, Dzidriņas, Upeslejas, Līči, Rumbula or Cekule. Ulbroka and Dreiliņi have pages of their own, while this one is about the parish as a whole and about what all its villages have in common.

A parish, not a village: the Stopiņi villages and their rules

Stopiņi parish is in Ropaži Municipality; from 2004 to 2021 there was a Stopiņi Municipality, so old documents and advertisements still call it that. The parish covers 53.5 km², and in 2026 it had 14,444 residents. In 2024 Saurieši had 1,866 residents, Dzidriņas 1,483, Upeslejas 1,355, Līči 1,146, Rumbula 503 and Cekule 200.

In 1975 the surroundings of the Jugla paper mill, Brekši, Franki, Jugla, Pļavnieki and part of Dreiliņi were handed to Riga, so the parish boundary runs right along Riga’s eastern neighbourhoods. For a house near this boundary it is worth making sure whether the address is in Riga or in Stopiņi parish: which municipality’s rules and which water management company apply depends on it.

In Stopiņi parish water management services are provided by the municipal company SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”, and the procedure for the services is set by Ropaži Municipality binding regulations No. 21/22. The company’s drinking water testing reports have separate points in Cekule, Dzidriņas, Kodes, Līči, Saurieši and Upeslejas, in Ulbroka and in Vālodzes; for Ulbroka, Saurieši, Cekule, Līči, Vālodzes and Dzidriņas they are reports of separate water supply systems.

A network service boundary act is attached to the water and sewerage service contract in Stopiņi parish. Point 2.5 of Ropaži Municipality binding regulations No. 21/22 defines it as an act signed by both parties that states the limits within which each party to the contract will maintain the water and sewer networks; by agreement the boundary may also lie outside the property boundary, and the act is prepared by the water management company (point 69). Before digging in the yard or replacing a pipe at the boundary it is worth finding this attachment among the contract papers.

The parish is crossed by the state roads A4 (the Riga bypass), A6, P2, P4 and P5, and by the Riga-Daugavpils railway with the stops Aconi, Saurieši and Rumbula; near Saurieši a gypsum quarry has operated since 1913. If you do not know how to name your village, the nearest station or road is enough.

Source: Ropaži Municipality binding regulations No. 21/22 “Procedure for providing and using public water management services in Ropaži Municipality”, points 2.5, 26, 26.1 and 69 (likumi.lv/ta/id/333680). Checked 24 November 2026.

Low relief, the Mazā Jugla and the Piķurga

The western part of the parish is in the Seaside Lowland and the eastern part in the Ropaži plain, and the height is only 4.7 to 16 m above sea level; about 54% of the territory is covered by forest. The Mazā Jugla flows along the eastern boundary, the Piķurga with its tributaries Dauguļupīte and Karātavgrāvis flows through the parish, and Lake Ulbroka is a 12 ha reservoir on the Piķurga.

In low relief the height matters for plumbing: basement fixtures, the yard pipe and the tank or street chamber can lie close to each other in height, and before a new pipe or a basement rebuild it is worth measuring rather than assuming. If water already appears in the basement, the page water in the basement tells what to do.

A lifting pump and sewerage in village houses

If the waste from a basement toilet or shower cannot drain by gravity to the outdoor pipe, a sewage lifting pump lifts it. When it starts to hum but does not pump, or switches on by itself, the work is described on the page sewage pump repair. If water does not go away because of a blockage, the pipe is cleared first; see the page drain unblocking.

What people in Stopiņi parish ask

I live in Saurieši (or Upeslejas). Does that count as Stopiņi?

Yes. Saurieši and Upeslejas are villages of Stopiņi parish in Ropaži Municipality, as are Dzidriņas, Līči, Rumbula, Cekule, Dreiliņi and Ulbroka. In the form you can write the name of your village; that and the street are enough for us.

I am building a house in Saurieši. Do I have to pay a one-off fee for wastewater treatment capacity?

Usually not. Amendments No. 4/25 to the Ropaži Municipality rules (in force from 11 February 2025) introduced a one-off fee for wastewater treatment capacity for new objects, but it applies to those who carry on economic activity, not to an ordinary private-house connection. If economic activity is planned in the house, it is worth clarifying when requesting the technical conditions.

In Līči the water pressure on the first floor of the house is weak. May I install my own pressure boost?

Point 26 of Ropaži Municipality regulations No. 21/22 provides for this: if more pressure is needed, the user installs local pressure boosting at their own expense, in agreement with the service provider. Before that it is worth checking whether the house’s own pipes are reducing the pressure, for example a narrowed old section or a half-closed valve.
