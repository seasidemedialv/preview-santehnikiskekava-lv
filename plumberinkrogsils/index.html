So plumbing work in Krogsils can start from zero: a new house, a new plot, a street owned by private persons. That is why a lot has to be decided before the floor, after the shell finish and before a purchase.

Detailed plans between the A7 and the P137

Krogsils is a village in Ķekava parish by the Ostvalds Canal between the A7 and P137 roads, with an area of 3.18 km². Its boundaries were approved in 2009 by merging Krogsils with Tīturgciems, a part of Alejas village, and the farmland and forest between them. Krogsils borders the town of Ķekava directly, while across the A7 lie Katlakalns, Alejas and Vimbukrogs.

The village assessment in the territorial plan (2019) says that most of Krogsils is not built up, and that the existing buildings are mostly detached houses and low-rise residential buildings. The detailed plans in force were mainly drawn up to divide plots. For plumbing this means houses built one after another on new plots, each with its own water and sewer solution.

The street up to your gate is not the municipality's

In Krogsils the municipality owns only the main streets, the rest belong to private persons, and the village assessment says this makes them harder to maintain. If a pipe or a trench has to run along such a street or across it, you have to talk to the owner of the street land, and this must be done before digging, not after.

The second question is access. On an unfinished or partly finished street, heavy machinery and material deliveries depend on the weather. If your street is gravel or not yet built to the end, say so in the request so that the work and deliveries can be planned.

New-build plumbing: what to decide before the floor

In a new house the cheapest moment for plumbing is before the floor is poured. That is when you decide where the WC, shower drain, basins, washing machine and kitchen will be, and from each spot the outlet runs under the floor to one point where the pipe leaves the house. Once the floor is poured, moving an outlet means cutting the concrete. We describe this work on the page about first-fix plumbing. Rough-in plumbing in a new house costs from €55 per connection point, pipes and fittings extra.

In Krogsils the spot where the sewer leaves the house is especially important. We do not plan on a street network as a given here (more on that in the section below), so if the house will have its own system, a septic tank or a biological unit, its place on the plot is decided together with the outlet. If the house is built to a design, the design sets the outlet position.

If a house was bought in shell finish with the outlets ready, the work is finishing the plumbing in a new build: connecting the fixtures, the water heater and the manifold. Its price depends on the number of fixtures and on whether the outlets match the plan. Fixing a basin with a trap and connection costs from €60 per sink. Before a purchase, a plumbing inspection of the house is worth having, to know what has already been done under the floor.

If the water in the new house will come from its own well, it is worth providing in the design a utility room where the pipe enters the house: that is where the main valve, filters, water heater and, with underfloor heating, its manifold stand. The room needs a floor drain and access to all the equipment. When this place is not provided, the water heater ends up in a cupboard or under the stairs, and every service gets harder.

The network and the municipal co-financing in Krogsils

Krogsils is not in the list of villages in the tariff zone published by SIA “Ķekavas nami” (Katlakalns, Krustkalni, Ķekava, Odukalns, Rāmava, Valdlauči, Ziedonis, Vimbukrogs, Daugmale). Ķekava Municipality co-financing for a connection is granted only if centralised water supply or sewer networks have already been built in the street or road along the property. A private person must have ownership rights in the land register and a declared place of residence in Ķekava Municipality on 1 January of the current year.

So on a street with no network, co-financing does not help the house, and the house has to be planned with its own system. The building’s internal plumbing is not covered by the support even where the network exists. More on the conditions on the page about co-financing for a connection.

Ķekava co-financing procedure (binding regulations No. 14/2023 and its amendments No. 3/2026): point 4.2 (networks already built along the property), point 4.3 (the internal system is not covered), point 6.1 (declared place of residence on 1 January), likumi.lv, checked on 24 November 2026.

Krogsils: questions from the building site

My street in Krogsils has no network yet: does the municipal co-financing apply to me?

Not until centralised networks have been built along your property. Ķekava Municipality regulations No. 14/2023 (point 4.2) tie co-financing to networks that are already in the street or road along the property. Until then the house needs its own water and sewer solution.

I am building a house in Krogsils: should the sewer outlet positions be decided before or after the floor is poured?

Before. The outlets from the WC, shower, basins and kitchen run under the floor, and after pouring they can be moved only by cutting the floor. Together with the outlets you also decide the place where the pipe leaves the house and goes to the septic tank or biological unit.

The documents say Tīturgciems: is that now Krogsils?

If it is about land between Krogsils and Alejas, most likely yes. In 2009 the boundaries of Krogsils were approved by merging it with Tīturgciems, a part of Alejas village, and the lands between them. The exact village is shown by the address in the address register.
