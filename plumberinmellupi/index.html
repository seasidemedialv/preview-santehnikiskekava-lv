The village’s 1.15 km² contain three quite different kinds of address: the Dārznieku and Dzintaru summer-house colonies, the historic centre by the V7 road, and farmsteads that take up the rest of the territory. Which of them your house is in decides how the supply line is laid, where the waste goes and even who owns the street at your gate.

A Mellupi address: what to say already in the request

In Mellupi the same fault in a colony house and on a farmstead needs different preparation. If the request has this information, the plumber knows what to bring, and the price can be named more exactly.

  1. Which part of the village The Dārznieku and Dzintaru summer-house colonies belong to Mellupi. By the V7 there is a historically formed centre with a petrol station, a timber-processing company, a shop, a cafe and a social centre, while outside the colonies and the centre farmsteads dominate. For a colony house we ask whether the pipes were laid for summer or for the whole year; for a farmstead, where the wastewater tank is and how far it is from the house.
  2. Who owns the street The street network of the garden cooperatives in Mellupi has passed into municipal ownership, but Dzērveņu iela belongs to AS “Latvijas valsts meži”. If a service pipe has to be dug in the street’s land, consent is asked of the street’s owner: the municipality for the cooperative streets, LVM for Dzērveņu iela. If the service pipe stays only on your own plot, the street owner usually does not have to be involved.
  3. Where the water comes from Mellupi does not appear in the “Ķekavas nami” tariff zones. It helps to know whether the house has its own well or borehole and whether the wastewater goes to a septic tank, a holding tank or a biological treatment unit.
  4. Where the sewage-removal receipts are If the tank or septic tank is emptied by a septic service, point 9.1 of Cabinet Regulation No. 384 requires the contracts or receipts to be kept for at least two years. They show how often the system is emptied, and it is worth keeping them in one folder with the registration notice of the system.

Keeping documents: Cabinet Regulation No. 384, point 9.1 (likumi.lv/ta/id/291947). Street owners and parts of the village: Ķekava Municipality village assessment (2019). Checked 24 November 2026.

Colony houses, farmsteads and the new detailed plan

The village assessment in the Ķekava Municipality territorial plan (2019) states that the summer-house building of the Mellupi garden cooperatives has partly turned into detached-house building. In a colony house where people now live in January too, the water pipe can freeze where it crosses an unheated crawl space or runs shallower in the ground; such cases are described on the page about frozen pipes and the supply line.

In 2009 the forests between the historic part of Mellupi and the cooperatives were also included within the village boundary, and in 2021 145 people lived on its 1.15 km². On a farmstead the route of water and waste is usually longer than in a colony: from the well or borehole to the house and from the house to the tank. A long yard section is where water can freeze first in winter and where the waste pipe blocks after years, so in the request it helps to say how many metres it is from the house to the tank and whether there is an inspection chamber on the way.

In the south-west of the village one detailed plan is in force, by which a property was divided into thirteen land units for residential building. If you are buying a house or land in this area, a plumbing check before buying is useful before the contract. If the pipes in an old colony house have to be replaced, the work is described on the page replacing pipes in a private house.

What people in Mellupi ask

On a farmstead in Mellupi there is a long yard pipe from the house to the tank. Where can it be cleaned from?

Through the inspection chamber or an inspection hatch at the house outlet, if there is one; if not, the pipe is cleaned from the house side. In a long pipe a blockage can form in dips and bends. Clearing a blockage in an outdoor drain costs from €50 per call-out with the first hour of work, and if the pipe blocks repeatedly, high-pressure flushing from €80 helps.

In a house in the Dārznieku colony in Mellupi the supply line freezes in winter. Where is it usually found?

Usually the culprit is the point where the pipe leaves the soil and passes into the house: under the floor, in an unheated crawl space or by the foundations, where frost reaches it from outside. The second place is a section buried shallower than it should be. The price of thawing depends on where the pipe is frozen and whether it has burst.

The sewage-removal receipts for a Mellupi house are lost. How long did they have to be kept?

Point 9.1 of Cabinet Regulation No. 384 requires the contracts or receipts for removing wastewater to be kept for at least two years. If they are lost, you can try asking the septic service for copies, and from now on keep them in one folder with the registration notice of the system.
